What is HydraFacial MD?

HydraFacial MD is the world's leading advanced facial treatment using patented Vortex-Fusion® technology — a multi-step process that deeply cleanses, extracts, and hydrates your skin simultaneously, without discomfort or downtime.

Unlike conventional facials, HydraFacial MD uses a unique spiral suction tip combined with proprietary serums to remove impurities while delivering powerful antioxidants, peptides, and hyaluronic acid deep into the skin.

01
Cleanse & Peel

Uncover a new layer of skin with gentle exfoliation and resurfacing.

02
Extract & Hydrate

Remove debris painlessly with automated vacuum suction while nourishing.

03
Fuse & Protect

Saturate skin with antioxidants and peptides to maximize glow.
