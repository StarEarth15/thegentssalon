What is HydraFacial MD?

HydraFacial MD is an advanced facial treatment built on patented Vortex-Fusion® technology. It cleanses, extracts and hydrates the skin in a single, comfortable session.

Rather than relying on harsh exfoliation, the treatment uses a spiral vortex tip and a range of targeted serums to clear impurities and deliver hydration and antioxidants into the skin.

01
Cleanse & Peel

Gentle exfoliation to refresh the surface of the skin.

02
Extract & Hydrate

Vortex suction clears debris while the skin is nourished.

03
Fuse & Protect

Antioxidant and hydrating serums are delivered into the skin.
